Cup Competition

How the cup works, and who each team plays in the group stage.

Group stage

Every team plays 4 of the other teams once each, across 5 match nights — so each team also gets one night off during the group stage. Cup fixtures are played in the same 9-game format as the league.

All results feed into a single combined table. Position is decided by games won (out of 9 per fixture). If teams are level, ties are broken in this order:

  1. Head-to-head result (where the tied teams have played each other)
  2. Number of matches won
  3. Leg difference (legs won minus legs lost, across every game)

Knockout rounds

Once the group stage finishes, the top 4 in the cup table go into the Knock Out Cup, and 5th–8th go into the Subsidiary Cup. Semi-final draws are fixed by league position rather than a random draw:

Knock Out Cup 1st v 4th, 2nd v 3rd

Subsidiary Cup 5th v 8th, 6th v 7th

In each tie the higher-placed team plays at home. Knockout matches are a single game — there's no second leg.
